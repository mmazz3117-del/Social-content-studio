Social Media Pal v1.9.5 — Business / General Test Mode

NEW
• Added a prominent Content Mode selector on Create.
• Business mode uses the saved business profile as before.
• General / Test mode isolates experimental content from the saved store profile.
• General / Test mode sends a neutral profile plus an explicit no-store/no-local-business instruction with the project brief.
• General / Test mode removes business/location footers from locally created Post and Story graphics and moving Story overlays.
• General / Test mode uses a neutral “Natural / Context-Aware” tone by default.
• Quick-start prompts change with the selected mode: store-oriented ideas in Business mode; story/moment ideas in General/Test mode.
• The selected Content Mode is remembered on the device.
• Recent Projects save and restore their Content Mode and show Business or General/Test in project metadata.
• Switching modes clears already-generated outputs but keeps uploaded media and the written brief, so the package can be rebuilt cleanly in the new mode.

KEPT FROM v1.9.4
• Motion-to-motion Reel transition system.
• Multi-video editing and Reel feedback controls.
• Tell Pal microphone controls.
• Preserve Reality media handling.

DEPLOYMENT
• Frontend only. No Firebase deployment required.
