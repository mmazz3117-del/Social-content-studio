SOCIAL MEDIA PAL v2.0 — CONTENT DIRECTOR

MAJOR COURSE CHANGE
- Removed automatic Reel/Story rendering and browser-based video editing.
- Rebuilt the app around planning, directing, shooting, assembling and publishing content.

NEW
- Reel / Short Video, Photo Post, Story and Full Content Plan formats.
- Business and General/Test modes retained.
- Director-style shot plans with exact shot type, framing, orientation, camera movement, timing and on-screen text.
- "Plan a new shoot" and "Work with what I have" workflows.
- Optional media upload for analysis only; original video is never rendered or edited.
- Shoot Mode walks through one shot at a time with progress, done checklist and a built-in shot timer.
- Assembly roadmap explains how to put the captured material together.
- Publish Kit organizes caption, shorter caption, cover text, Story copy, CTA and hashtags.
- Plan refinements: Another idea, More detailed, Simplify, More fun.
- Recent Plans save the creative roadmap for later review.
- Tell Pal voice brief and microphone modes retained.

DEPLOYMENT
- Frontend-only update. Replace index.html, app.js and styles.css in GitHub Pages.
- No Firebase Functions redeploy is required for v2.0.
